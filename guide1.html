How to Buy a Home with 3.5% Down in Texas

A complete breakdown of FHA loan requirements in Texas, including credit benchmarks, income audits, and how to utilize down payment assistance programs.

By Robin Thomas FHA & Down Payment 6 min read

For most first-time buyers, the down payment is the single biggest hurdle standing between renting and owning. The FHA loan program was built to solve exactly that problem — and in Texas, where home prices vary widely between Houston, Sugar Land, and the surrounding suburbs, it remains one of the most accessible paths to homeownership.

Here's exactly how the program works, what lenders look for, and how to stack it with down payment assistance to get into a home with less cash out of pocket than most buyers expect.

What Is an FHA Loan?

An FHA loan is a mortgage insured by the Federal Housing Administration and issued through approved private lenders like Edge Home Finance. Because the government backs a portion of the risk, lenders can approve borrowers with lower credit scores and smaller down payments than most conventional loans allow.

Credit Benchmarks Lenders Use

Your credit score determines exactly how much you'll need to put down:

  • 580 credit score or higher — qualifies for the minimum 3.5% down payment.
  • 500–579 credit score — still eligible, but the required down payment increases to 10%.
  • Below 500 — typically not eligible for FHA financing.

Lenders will also review your credit history for recent late payments, collections, and overall debt load — not just the score itself.

How Income Audits Work

FHA underwriting looks closely at your debt-to-income (DTI) ratio — the percentage of your gross monthly income that goes toward debt payments. Most FHA lenders look for:

  • A front-end ratio (housing costs alone) around 31% or less.
  • A back-end ratio (all debts combined) around 43%, though some borrowers qualify above that with compensating factors.
  • Two years of consistent employment or income history, verified through pay stubs, W-2s, or tax returns.
Good to Know

FHA loans also allow gift funds from family members to cover part or all of your down payment — something many conventional loan programs restrict.

Down Payment Assistance Programs in Texas

Texas offers several programs that can be paired with an FHA loan to reduce your upfront costs even further, including:

  • TSAHC (Texas State Affordable Housing Corporation) — grants and second-lien assistance for eligible buyers.
  • TDHCA My First Texas Home — down payment and closing cost assistance paired with a low fixed-rate first mortgage.
  • Local city and county programs available in Houston, Harris County, and Fort Bend County.

Combined correctly, these programs can reduce the cash you need at closing to well under the standard 3.5%.

A Realistic Timeline

1. Pre-Approval

We review your credit, income, and assets to confirm what you qualify for — typically completed within 24-48 hours.

2. House Hunting

With a pre-approval letter in hand, you shop with confidence and a clear budget.

3. Underwriting & Closing

Once you're under contract, underwriting typically takes 2-3 weeks, followed by closing.

Is an FHA Loan Right for You?

FHA loans aren't the right fit for every buyer — if your credit score is strong and you have 20% saved, a conventional loan may cost less over time. But for buyers who need flexibility on credit or down payment, it remains one of the most reliable paths into a Texas home.
