Meet Robin Thomas

Mortgage guidance rooted in Houston, built on trust.

For over a decade, I've helped Houston-area families and investors get financed — from a first apartment-to-homeowner jump to complex investment property portfolios. I started SmartNest Mortgage because too many buyers were getting a single rate quote and calling it their only option.

As a licensed mortgage broker, I'm not a loan officer at one bank. I work across a network of 120+ lending partners to compare programs, rates, and terms, then match you with the option that actually fits your income, timeline, and goals — explained in plain language, at every step.

My Journey

How I ended up on the buyer's side of the table.

A quick look at the path from loan officer to independent broker — and why I made the switch.

01 / 2013

Started in Lending

Began my career as a loan officer, working directly with one bank's products and rate sheet — and seeing firsthand how limiting that could be for buyers.

02 / 2016

Went Independent

Became a licensed mortgage broker so I could shop rates and programs across multiple lenders instead of offering just one institution's terms.

03 / 2019

Founded SmartNest Mortgage

Built a brokerage focused on plain-language guidance for Houston-area buyers, refinancers, and investors — no jargon, no pressure.

04 / Today

500+ Families Financed

Still hands-on with every file, still comparing across a growing lender network for each client's specific situation.
